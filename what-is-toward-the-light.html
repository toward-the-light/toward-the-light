What is Toward the Light!?

Toward the Light! – A Message to Mankind from the Transcendental World is a book unlike any other. It was first published in Copenhagen in 1920 by Michael Agerskov (1870–1933), a Danish literature teacher and writer, and has since been translated into many languages.

According to its publishers, the book was not written by human authors. It was received over several years through intuition and inspiration by Agerskov's wife, Johanne Agerskov (1873–1946), who served as intermediary for spiritual intelligences who wished to give humanity answers to its deepest questions.

Read it through. Reflect. Then let your own conscience be the judge. — the only request made of the reader, Preface 1920

The questions it addresses

  • Who are we, and where do we come from?
  • Was the human being created by God?
  • Why do evil, suffering and death exist, if God is love?
  • Do we survive death — and have we lived before?
  • What did Jesus actually teach, and what was later added or distorted?
  • How did the universe come into being — and what lies beyond it?

What the book contains

Ardor's Account

A historical account from the very beginning to our own time: God's arising as a personal Being, the creation of the universe and the angels, the fall of the “Eldest” under the power of Darkness, and how that fall led to the creation of human beings. It describes how God, out of infinite love, adopted the fallen ones' creatures as His own children and established the law of reincarnation, so that every human spirit may mature across many earthly lives. It also retells decisive events in human history from a transcendental perspective — above all the life of Jesus of Nazareth.

The Speech of Christ and the Speech of God's Servant

Two ethical-religious addresses in which Christ speaks directly to every human being — of the Father's laws, of prayer, of forgiveness, and of living together in tolerance, peace and love.

The Parables

Six parables — including The Two Brothers, The Roads, and The Prince and the Poor Men — that carry the book's message in story form. Many readers meet the book for the first time through a parable.

The Commentary

Detailed explanations of the spiritual and physical dimensions, the “spheres” where human spirits dwell between incarnations, prehistoric civilizations such as Atlantis, the laws behind so-called occult phenomena, and the structure of the universe.

The Postscript

The remarkable account of how the book came into being — the séances, the circle of seven participants, and the years of work behind the text.

The essence of the message

  • In the dawn of time, God's fallen children created the visible human body; God is the Father of the invisible human spirit.
  • Every earthly life brings the human spirit one step closer to the Light and to the Father's home.
  • Every human being is accompanied by invisible guardians, whose help is perceived as an inner voice — the conscience.
  • Through prayer, every person can reach God directly and receive strength for the struggle against Darkness and sin.
  • Each of us must atone for our own wrongful thoughts and deeds — but God's help is always there for the asking.
  • Christ has promised to be the guide of every human being until all earthly lives are complete.

Is this a religion or a sect?

No. Toward the Light! founds no church, no congregation and no membership. It explains how and why religions arose — and invites every person, regardless of creed, race or nationality, to a direct and personal relationship with God. There is nothing to join, nothing to pay. The book is free.
