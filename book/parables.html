Parables

Addendum

  • The Two BrothersTwo brothers on a stony path under a burning sun — about not leaving a brother behind, and about Jesus and Joseph of Arimathea.
  • The Woodland PondStill, clear water mirrors the sky more truly than any human painting — an image of the quiet mind that reflects the divine.
  • The RoadsA traveller asks which road is the right one — the Father’s ways are many, yet all who follow their calling in hope and trust reach His Kingdom.
  • The Prince and the Poor MenA prince, a beggar and a faithful gardener — “you were faithful in that which was least, you will be faithful also in much.”
  • The Fruit SeedsA precious seed is lost and patiently sought — the parable refers to the downfall and return of the Elder (Ardor).
  • Uni and EsarTwo servants sent to win back a divided city — how the Father weighs the self-righteous against the just of heart.
