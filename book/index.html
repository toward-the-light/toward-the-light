Ardor's Account — a historical presentation from the earliest times to our day

  • Ardor's Account
  • 1. Has God Existed through all Eternity, and is God a Personal Being?
  • 2. How did the Angels Enter the Kingdom of God, and what is their Relationship to God?
  • 3. How and for what Purpose did God Create the Globes of the Universe?
  • 4. Were Human Beings Created by God? From where and for what Reason have Sin and Death Come to Afflict the Human Race?
  • 5. Have Human Beings no Right then to Call themselves Children of God?
  • 6. Are the many Earthly Lives of Human Beings Predetermined by God?
  • 7. In what Manner were the Spiritual Bodies of God’s Children Bound to Human Bodies? And in what way did the Youngest Bring the Light unto Mankind?
  • 8. How did the Eldest Look upon the Work of the Youngest for the Benefit of Humanity?
  • 9. Did the Eldest Continue to be Invisible to Human Beings? Were none of them Incarnated on Earth?
  • 10. Was the Birth of Jesus Supernatural? Was he Conceived by the Holy Ghost?
  • 11. What were Jesus’ own Thoughts Concerning his Mission on Earth?
  • 12. How are we to Understand the Baptism of Jesus?
  • 13. How should we Interpret the Temptation in the Wilderness?
  • 14. Did Jesus Teach of an Eternal Hell? Did he Teach Nothing more of the Kingdom of Heaven than what is already Known through the Gospels?
  • 15. Did Jesus Perform Miracles? Did Jesus Heal the Sick?
  • 16. How should we Interpret the Transfiguration on the Mount?
  • 17. What was the Relationship of Jesus to the Apostles?
  • 18. Was Jesus always Ready when he was Called unto the Sick and the Grieving?
  • 19. Did Jesus Speak on the Significance of Baptism with the Words Ascribed to him by Tradition?
  • 20. Why did Jesus Refuse to Baptize the People who Sought him?
  • 21. Why did Jesus then Allow the Apostles to Baptize, when he himself Refused to do this?
  • 22. How did the High Priests and the Scribes Look upon the Works of Jesus?
  • 23. Was Joseph of Arimathea able to Assist Jesus in his Work?
  • 24. Did Jesus Foretell the End of the World and his „Second Coming“ in the Manner Handed down by Tradition?
  • 25. Was it Predetermined that Judas should Betray Jesus? With what Purpose and with what Words did Jesus Institute the Holy Communion?
  • 26. What were the Words Spoken by Jesus when he Stood Accused before the Council?
  • 27. What were the Words Spoken by Jesus before he Died on the Cross?
  • 28. Was the Resurrection of Jesus Bodily or Spiritual?
  • 29. How many Times did Jesus Appear before the Apostles? Did he Speak to them?
  • 30. How should we Interpret the „Miracle of Pentecost“?
  • 31. Were the Apostles able to Proclaim the Teaching of Jesus as Truly as they had Received it?
  • 32. How was Saul Converted from Persecutor to Preacher of the Teachings of Jesus?
  • 33. In what Manner did the Doctrine of Atonement Arise?
  • 34. Did the Light Make no Advance against Darkness with the further Spread of Christianity?
  • 35. Did the Youngest not seek to Counter the False Teaching that the Elder had Instilled in the Minds of Human Beings?
  • 36. Did the Spirits of the Dead Continue to Walk among Human Beings, or did God Gradually Succeed in Calling back all of them?
