The Book › Commentary to Ardor's Account

Section XXIX

In his Account Ardor points out that when Jesus appeared before the apostles he was standing at the upper end of the table. Jesus chose this place so that all should know that he had not come to them through the entrance door in the customary way.

Jesus appeared before the apostles with God’s permission and by the power of his own will, thus without the intervention of a medium, although still materialized according to his will.

The ascension of Jesus is legend.
