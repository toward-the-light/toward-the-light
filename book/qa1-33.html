33.

Is Martin Luther’s “vision” at Wartburg more than myth? Besides being a reformer, was it also Luther’s task to pray for Satan?

Luther’s “vision” of Satan was real. He was one of those Youngest who had pledged to remember the prayer for Ardor while they were incarnated. But he also1 misunderstood the promptings of the guardian spirit and rather than praying for Satan, he cursed him, with the result that Ardor gained yet greater power over him.
