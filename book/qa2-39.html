39.

Was there any special reason—for example, the Earth’s location—why God chose this globe1 as the habitat for His intended beings of Light?

Yes, many special reasons, but a more detailed explanation2 will not be given here. Only this can be said: the Earth’s location in the universe was one of the main reasons.

Notes

  1. Toward the Light, page 6: 5 to page 7: 2.↩
  2. A more detailed explanation will not be given because humans themselves should be able to arrive at some of the reasons for the choice of the Earth.↩
