Questions & Answers

Who wrote Toward the Light!?

No human author claims the text. It was published in 1920 by the Danish teacher and writer Michael Agerskov, who states in his preface that the work came into being through intuition and inspiration via his wife, Johanne Agerskov, as intermediary for spiritual intelligences. The circle around them documented the process, described openly in the book's Postscript.

Is Toward the Light a religion, a church or a sect?

No. There is no congregation, no membership, no clergy and nothing to join. The book explains how and why the religions of the world arose, and points each individual toward a direct, personal relationship with God. The international foundation exists solely to make the book known and available.

Is this spiritualism?

The book itself answers this firmly. Although it was received through a medium, it contains an explicit warning against spiritualism and against calling the dead. The contact, it explains, was initiated by the transcendental world — not the other way around. Johanne Agerskov wrote in 1939 that those who dismiss the work as “spiritualism” merely reveal that they have not read it.

What does the book say about God?

That God is a personal Being — the Father of every human spirit — whose nature is love, justice and compassion. It rejects the ideas of eternal damnation and of a God who demands fear, and invites each person to seek God directly in prayer.

What does it say about Jesus?

A large part of the book retells the life of Jesus from a transcendental perspective and presents his teaching “freed from the inventions and distortions of the centuries.” Christ is portrayed as the eldest of God's youngest children, who has promised to guide every human being until all their earthly lives are complete.

Does the book teach reincarnation?

Yes. Reincarnation is presented as God's law of justice and growth: each human spirit matures across many earthly lives, and each must atone for its own wrongful deeds — yet always with God's help available for the asking. Eternal life is described as an irrevocable gift to every human being.

Is the book free? Why?

Yes — completely. The full text is free to read online and to download as PDF, in several languages. A print edition is available at cost for those who prefer a physical book.

Who is behind this website?

toward-the-light.org is an English-language presentation of the book, maintained in cooperation with the international Vandrer mod Lyset! foundation. The official publisher is Toward the Light Publishing House ApS, Copenhagen. Related sites: phll.org (Spanish) · vandrer-mod-lyset.dk (Danish).

How should I approach the book if I'm skeptical?

Exactly as its publisher asked in 1920: read it closely and in full, weigh every thought, and then let your own conscience be the judge. Skepticism is natural; the book asks only for an honest reading. Our 30-minute reading path is a good way to test the waters.
