What is this book?

Toward the Light! was received between 1912 and 1918 by a small Danish circle around Johanne and Michael Agerskov, who stated that the text was conveyed from the transcendental world — not authored by any human hand. It speaks of God as a loving Father, of the eternal life of every human spirit, of reincarnation as God's law of justice and growth, and of Christ's true teaching, “freed from the inventions and distortions of the centuries.”

It is not a church, not a sect, and not a movement. There is nothing to join. There is only a book — and an invitation to read it.
